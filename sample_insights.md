# Example Insights

1. States with higher pending volume may indicate staffing imbalance or slower document collection cycles
2. Analysts with high turnaround time but high volume may need process support rather than performance intervention
3. Missing document cases are a strong driver of backlog growth and should be monitored weekly
4. SLA breach tracking helps leadership prioritize high-risk files before network delays increase
5. Specialty-level trends can help forecast seasonal or operational workload changes
